# AI Tools for Business Research

**NUS Business School · Dean's PhD and Research Office**
**4 & 5 August 2026 · 09:00–17:00 SGT · Biz 2, Level 5, Seminar Room 05-11**

### → [shao-jiaqi.github.io/ai-workshop-2026](https://shao-jiaqi.github.io/ai-workshop-2026/)

A two-day hands-on workshop in which you build your own AI research ecosystem — and
leave with a research companion that keeps learning from your work.

---

## Start here

**[Setup](https://shao-jiaqi.github.io/ai-workshop-2026/setup.html)** — 30 to 45 minutes,
before Tuesday morning. Two accounts, three installs, one check that it works. The two
days are compressed, so we verify your environment together in Session 1B rather than
building it from scratch.

Also bring **a small piece of your own data, or one research chore you would like to
automate**. The practices work far better on a problem you actually care about.

**No prior programming experience is required.** The second half of Session 1 is a
from-zero primer. If you already work in Python, R, or Stata, treat it as a
setup-verification pass.

## Practices

**[Practice guide](materials/practice/README.md)** — install the kit, then A, B, C.

Three hands-on blocks, twenty minutes each, all on the same paper. They compound: A gets
you a verified reading, B turns it into two deliverables, C turns it into knowledge that
keeps paying back.

| | Practice | Session |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | [Set up, then understand the paper](materials/practice/A-first-session/PA_practice.pdf) | 1B |
| B | [Paper to talk, then to referee](materials/practice/B-paper-to-slides/PB_practice.pdf) | 2 |
| C | [Make the paper compound](materials/practice/C-llm-wiki/PC_practice.pdf) | 3A |

Everything runs out of [`materials/practice/claude-setup/`](materials/practice/claude-setup/) —
project memory, a faithfulness rule, a house Beamer style, the `paper-to-slides` skill,
and a `referee` subagent. Adapted from
[Pedro Sant'Anna's academic workflow](https://psantanna.com/claude-code-my-workflow/).

> **The practice paper is handed out in the room.** All three practices run on one
> field-experiment manuscript that is unpublished, so it is not distributed here. You get
> it at the start of Session 1B. Everything else you need is in this repository, so you
> can read ahead.

## The two days

Twelve teaching hours. Running order is fixed; clock times are indicative.

**Day one — Tuesday 4 August.** 1A · Evolution of code and AI risk (Wang) → 1B · Getting
started (Shao, *Practice A*) → 2 · Claude Code at research scale: vibe coding, worktrees,
personalisation (Shao, *Practice B*).

**Day two — Wednesday 5 August.** 3A · Obsidian + Claude Code (Shao, *Practice C*) →
3B · Literature review and ideation (Wang) → 4A · Data collection and alternative data
(Wang) → 4B · Identification and research design (Wang).

Full detail, with times and session descriptions, is on
[the site](https://shao-jiaqi.github.io/ai-workshop-2026/#days).
