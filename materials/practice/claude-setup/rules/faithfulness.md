# Rule — Faithfulness (non-negotiable)

This paper will be presented and refereed. A single invented number destroys trust.

## Do
- Trace **every** number and claim to a **section or table** in the paper. Prefer "−4.7 pp (Table 3)" over "about five percent".
- When you summarize, quote the paper's own framing (e.g. "disciplinary vs. psychological").
- If the paper does not report something, say **"not reported"** — do not estimate it.
- Distinguish what the paper *shows* from what it *claims* / *interprets*.

## Don't
- Never fabricate a coefficient, sample size, table number, citation, or p-value.
- Never round a headline number without keeping the exact one nearby.
- Never present the referee's opinion as the paper's result, or vice versa.

## Self-check before finishing
- Could I point to the exact table for each number on the page? If not, remove or flag it.
- Does any slide/page assert something the paper only speculates about?
